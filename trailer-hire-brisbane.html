Why choose trailer hire in Brisbane over a van or ute?

For most moves, tip runs and weekend projects, a box trailer is the most affordable way to carry bulky items. You keep driving your own car, there is no extra driver to book, and you only pay for the days you need.

  • Use your own car and tow bar
  • No extra driver or delivery fee
  • Collect and return any time, day or night
  • Discounted rates for multi day hires

A trailer gives you the space of a ute with the flexibility of your own schedule.

Before you tow

  • Make sure your vehicle has a suitable tow bar and compatible trailer plug
  • Check your vehicle's towing capacity in the owner's manual
  • Confirm the coupling is locked, the safety chain is attached and the lights work
  • Allow extra braking distance and take corners wider than usual

Loading tips for a safe trip

  • Place the heaviest items low and slightly forward of the axle
  • Spread the weight evenly from side to side
  • Restrain every item with ratchet straps or rope
  • Cover and secure loose loads such as green waste, soil and mulch

How much does trailer hire cost in Brisbane?

Pricing depends on the trailer size and how many days you need it. Your full total is shown before you confirm, and multi day hires are discounted. Contact us for a quick quote.

Can I pick up a trailer after hours?

Yes. Both our Upper Kedron and Gaythorne locations offer 24/7 contactless pickup and return using secure access details sent when you book.

What size trailer do I need to move house?

For most house and unit moves the 8x5 caged box trailer is the best choice. For smaller loads, tip runs and DIY supplies the 7x5 is easier to tow and manoeuvre.

Do I need a special licence to tow a box trailer?

A standard Queensland car licence (class C) generally covers towing a light box trailer, provided your vehicle has a suitable tow bar and is rated to tow the load.

Can I use trailer hire in Brisbane for green waste and tip runs?

Absolutely. Our galvanised trailers are ideal for green waste, hard rubbish and tip runs. Please make sure loads are secured and covered when required.

How long is a hire day?

Each hire day is a full 24 hours from your pickup time, so you are not rushing to return before closing time.
