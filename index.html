Ask a Question
What trailers can I hire from No Strings Attached?

We offer trailer hire in Upper Kedron and Gaythorne. Our fleet includes 8x5 and 7x5 galvanised caged box trailers, perfect for moving, projects and weekend getaways.

How does trailer hire work per day?

Our daily hire period is 24 hours. You will see your total before confirming your booking. Rates vary by trailer and longer hires are discounted.

Where do I collect my trailer?

Pick up your trailer at Upper Kedron or Gaythorne. Book online, receive your secure access details and collect your trailer at your convenience. Call 0447 943 160 for assistance.

How do I rent a trailer?

Select your trailer, location and dates to book online in minutes. You will see all availability and your total before confirming. Need help? Email bodhi@lockii.app or call 0447 943 160.

Are there discounts for longer hires?

Yes. We offer discounts for multi day and extended trailer hires. The total depends on the trailer and duration, so check your dates on the booking form or contact us for details.

Need help picking a trailer?

If you are not sure which trailer is right for your job, just ask. Our team is happy to help you choose the best fit.
